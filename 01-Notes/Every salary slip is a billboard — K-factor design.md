# Every salary slip is a billboard — K-factor design

> Distribution baked into the artifact, not bolted onto onboarding.

## Context
The cheapest growth channel is the one where the user generates impressions on your behalf as a byproduct of using the product. For an HR/payroll product, the salary slip is the natural artifact:

- Average shop = 12 employees
- 12 employees × 12 months = **144 impressions/year per shop**
- Many employees are owner-relatives running their own small businesses
- 98% WhatsApp open rate vs 22% email

Every "Powered by Arth Saathi" slip sent to an employee is an impression to a potential future owner.

## Design implications
- Slip must be share-ready (WhatsApp-formatted, no friction)
- Watermark visible, not buried
- Employee app onboarding (when they download to view the slip) is the **second viral hop**
- K-factor target: ≥1.3 (each user brings >1 new user)

## Why this beats paid acquisition
SalaryBox spends ₹1,800/mo per customer to acquire. Khatabook spent ₹20M buying free downloads. **Artifact-baked virality has zero marginal CAC** once you build it once.

## Generalization
This pattern works for any product where the user's output is naturally shared with others:
- Invoice tools → invoices ("Powered by Refrens")
- Calendly → meeting bookings
- Linktree → bio links
- Notion → public pages

Ask: *what does my user create that they hand to someone else?*
