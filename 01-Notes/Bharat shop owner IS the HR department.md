# Bharat shop owner IS the HR department

> Every existing HR product assumes there is an HR person reading email on a laptop. In Bharat, there isn't.

## Context
The Indian shop owner is the bookkeeper, the recruiter, the disciplinarian, the cashier, and the WhatsApp customer-service line — *all of it, on one phone, between customers*. Building "HR software" for them is a category error. They need a friend on WhatsApp, not an admin panel.

## Implication
Software UX must:
- Live on a 6-inch phone, not a laptop
- Default to chat/voice, not forms
- Assume the user is mid-transaction with a customer when they open the app
- Never ask them to "set up" the product (no logo upload, no role hierarchy JSON, no leave-policy designer)
