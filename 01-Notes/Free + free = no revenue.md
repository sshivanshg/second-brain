# Free + free = no revenue

> Khatabook proved this with 10M MAU. Free distribution + free product + bolt-on premium = customers who never pay.

## Context
The trap: get to massive distribution on a free product, then layer paid features on top. Sounds reasonable. Doesn't work because:

1. Users self-selected on free → willingness to pay was never tested
2. Adding a paywall to features they already had = perceived theft, not value
3. The free product set a price anchor of ₹0; ₹500 feels like ∞

## What actually works
**Monetize unlocked *capabilities*, not seats.** A 10-person shop pays ₹500/mo when they need WhatsApp salary auto-send and UPI disbursement — not when they hire the 11th employee. The unlock matches an actual workflow pain they wanted solved.

Capability-tied pricing means:
- The free tier is genuinely useful (so users adopt the daily habit)
- The paid tier solves a *different* problem (not "more of the same")
- The user pays because they asked for the feature, not because they hit a quota

## Evidence
- Khatabook: 10M MAU on free ledger → couldn't monetize → tried Pagarkhata payroll (free) → killed 2025
- PagarBook: $20M spent buying free downloads → couldn't convert → 85% down-round
- SalaryBox: kept the floor at ₹1,800 (no free tier) → grew slow but real
- Kredily: true ₹0 forever + DPIIT subsidy → high free MAU, very low paid conversion

## Why subscription works *now* and didn't for Khatabook
**UPI AutoPay crossed 1.27B mandates in Nov 2025.** Recurring billing in Bharat finally works. Khatabook tried this 3 years too early.
