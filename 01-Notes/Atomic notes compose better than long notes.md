# Atomic notes compose better than long notes

> Small, single-idea notes can be remixed across many contexts; long notes lock ideas to one structure.

## Context
The Zettelkasten insight: ideas gain value through unexpected connections. A single 5000-word doc on "Productivity" buries 30 distinct ideas under one heading. Thirty atomic notes, each with their own links, let those ideas meet each other.

## Details
- One idea per note, titled as a claim ("X causes Y", not "About X").
- Long notes are write-once, read-rarely. Atomic notes get linked into many contexts.
- The graph view becomes useful only when notes are atomic.
