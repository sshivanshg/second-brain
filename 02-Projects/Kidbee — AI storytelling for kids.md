# 🐝 Kidbee — AI-Powered Storytelling for Kids

> **One-liner:** Educational platform that uses AI to create interactive, personalized stories for children — multi-language, voice narration, animated videos, parental controls.

**GitHub:** [sshivanshg/kidai](https://github.com/sshivanshg/kidai) · Backend: `kidbee-backend` (fork, private)

## For kids
- **AI Story Generation** from simple prompts
- **Animated Videos** with AI-generated visuals
- **Voice Narration** in multiple voices
- **Multi-Language** — 10+ Indian languages
- **Favorites & Library** to save/organize

## For parents
- **Parent Dashboard** — manage children's accounts
- **Parent PIN Protection** — gate sensitive features
- **Story Approval** — review before publish
- **Progress Tracking** — child activity

## Stack
- React 19
- TypeScript 5
- Bun (Node.js runtime)
- MongoDB

## Relationship to TinyTales
[[TinyTales StoryGlow]] and Kidbee are sister projects — same problem space (personalized AI storytelling for kids) but different angles:
- **TinyTales** — photo + theme → storybook + animated video (visual personalization, single-story output)
- **Kidbee** — broader edu platform (library, multi-language, parental controls)

Worth reconciling: is one the production version of the other? Or two distinct bets?
