# Pricing tiers

| Tier | Price | Headcount | Includes |
|------|-------|-----------|----------|
| **Starter** | ₹0/mo | ≤10 emp | AI chat, attendance, salary, employee app |
| **Growth** | ₹500/mo | 10–25 emp | + WhatsApp auto-send, PDF slips, GPS, UPI disbursement |
| **Scale** | ₹1,000/mo | 25+ emp | + multi-branch, reports, Aadhaar eKYC, priority support |

## Pricing principle
**We don't charge per seat. We charge per unlocked capability.** A 10-person shop pays ₹500/mo when they need WhatsApp + UPI — not when they hire the 11th employee. This matches actual willingness-to-pay.

## Why Khatabook's free model didn't work for them
Khatabook hit 10M MAU on a free ledger and could never monetize because:
1. Ledger is a monthly product — no daily habit
2. Bolt-on premium asked the user to value features they'd already received for free
3. No fintech rail attached

Arth Saathi's free tier exists to **build the daily attendance habit**. Monetization comes from capability unlocks the owner *asks for* (WhatsApp slips, UPI disbursement) — not from a paywall surprise.
