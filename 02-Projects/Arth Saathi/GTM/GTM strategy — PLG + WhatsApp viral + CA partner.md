# GTM strategy — PLG + WhatsApp viral + CA partner

## Three motion stack

### 1. Product-led growth (PLG)
- Free Starter + 5-min onboarding (phone OTP)
- Target: **CAC <₹150**, Day-1 active **>40%**
- Khatabook's playbook applied to staff/payroll (which has daily habit, unlike ledger)

### 2. WhatsApp viral loop (K-factor engine)
- Every salary slip = "Powered by Arth Saathi" billboard
- 10–50 employees per shop; many are owner-relatives
- Target: **K-factor ≥1.3**
- 98% WhatsApp open rate vs 22% email
- See [[Every salary slip is a billboard — K-factor design]]

### 3. CA partner + ground sales
- ₹500/shop referral to CAs, accountants, CAIT chapters
- Cities: **Surat, Ludhiana, Coimbatore, Jaipur, Indore**
- Year 1 target: **1,000 CA partners**
- Year 2 target: **5,000 CA partners**

## Demand-gen surface (live)

**SEO constellation** — 10+ landing pages targeting BoFU search:
- ai-hr-assistant
- attendance-management-app
- gps-attendance-app
- payroll-software
- leave-management-system
- salary-slip-generator
- whatsapp-payroll
- khata-app
- inventory-management
- billing-software

**Conversion:** 14-day Razorpay trial. Founder-grantable premium windows for pilots.

## Why all three are needed
- **PLG alone:** SalaryBox plateau (no Series A in 4.5y)
- **CA channel alone:** Slow, expensive ground game
- **Viral alone:** Without product-market fit you're amplifying churn

Combined: PLG gets you to 5K shops, CA partners + viral take you to 100K.
