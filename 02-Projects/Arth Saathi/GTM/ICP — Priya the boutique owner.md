# ICP — Priya, the Boutique Owner

> The early-adopter persona. Every product decision should be defensible by reference to her.

## Profile
- **Age:** 35–50
- **Location:** Tier 2/3 (Surat, Ludhiana, Coimbatore, Jaipur, Indore)
- **Headcount:** 5–30 staff
- **Language:** Hinglish-comfortable (Hindi-Punjabi-Gujarati mix)
- **Devices:** WhatsApp + UPI native; primary business device is her phone
- **Pain:** Loses 2–3 hrs/month to salary disputes; scared of labour inspector
- **Budget:** Can't afford ₹6,999/mo (Keka); already uses Khatabook free

## What she actually does today
- Writes attendance in a paper notebook
- Settles salary disputes by re-reading last month's notebook
- Pays staff in cash at end of month
- Outsources PF/ESI to a CA she doesn't fully understand
- Coordinates leaves over a chaotic WhatsApp group

## What "delight" looks like for her
- Says *"Ramesh ki haziri laga do, aaj half day"* → it's done, no form
- Salary day: one tap → WhatsApp slips sent → UPI auto-disburses → done
- Employee asks for advance → app pops the request → she taps Approve → money moves
- Year-end Form 16 generates itself

## What kills the trial in 7 days
- Form-heavy onboarding (logo upload, role hierarchy JSON, leave-policy setup)
- English-only UI
- Wrong attendance marked once
- Surprise paywall
