# The Enemy — what we're displacing

> We don't primarily compete with apps. We compete with the **established behaviors** that keep the owner stuck.

## The 5 enemies

1. **The notebook.** Every Bharat shop has one — handwritten attendance, salary advances scrawled in margins, disputes settled by re-reading last month's pages.
2. **The WhatsApp group.** Owner + employees in one group: *"kal chutti chahiye sir"*, *"advance de do"*, *"salary kab milegi"*. No structure, no audit trail, no compliance.
3. **The harassed CA.** Monthly visit, Excel sheet, ₹500–2,000/mo fee, files PF/ESI when forced, "manages" compliance reactively. Owner trusts the CA but resents the bill.
4. **The abandoned Keka trial.** ~30% of motivated owners *try* an HRMS once, abandon within 7 days because it asks for too much: logo upload, role hierarchy, leave-policy JSON, English-only forms.
5. **The free apps that never monetized.** Pagarbook, Pagarkhata, Khatabook free ledger, SalaryBook free trial — owner downloads, uses 2 months, hits paywall surprise, churns to next free app.

## Job-to-be-done

> Not "buy HR software." It is **"stop spending 2 hours/month arguing about Ramesh's attendance, and stop being scared of the labour inspector."**
