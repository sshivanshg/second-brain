# Inflection triggers — why now

> Six independent shifts that all converged in 2025–26. The window opens here and likely closes by ~2028.

## The 6 triggers

### 1. Aadhaar eKYC for private-sector attendance (Jan 2025)
Private companies can now use Aadhaar for employee verification. Previously gov-only. Unlocks legit on-boarding flows that don't require physical paperwork.

### 2. Labour Codes 2020 go live nationwide (April 2026)
Wages-as-% of CTC must be ≥50%. Statutory by-7th-of-month rule. Every 5–50 employee shop will need software they can't currently afford. Forced compliance = forced TAM expansion.

### 3. UPI AutoPay crosses 1.27B mandates (Nov 2025)
Recurring billing finally works in Bharat. ₹500/mo subscriptions are collectable. This didn't exist when Khatabook tried.

### 4. DPDP Act enforcement (Nov 2026 onwards)
Consent + retention + erasure paths mandatory. Manual notebook can't comply. Software-driven audit trail becomes the default.

### 5. Vernacular LLM cost + quality crossover
Cost collapsed **94.5% in 36 months**. Claude Opus 4.5 hits **77% BFCL** (tool-calling). Sarvam Saaras V3 hits **19% WER on Hinglish**. Voice agent is finally affordable + good enough.

### 6. Jio Bharat 5G @ ₹3,999 (2025)
Converts last 250M feature-phone users to smartphone users. Smartphone-first product captures them on day one.

## Why this matters
Each one alone is small. **Stacked**, they erase the obstacles that killed Pagarbook and Pagarkhata in 2020–2022 — and they're all happening in the same 18-month window.
