# Competitors — landscape and gaps

## Direct + adjacent players

| Player | Positioning | Why it loses | Gap Arth owns |
|--------|-------------|--------------|---------------|
| **SalaryBox** (YC) | Form-based GPS attendance + payroll | No Series A in 4.5y; English-mixed UI; ₹1,800/mo floor | ₹0 entry + Hindi voice |
| **Kredily** | True ₹0 forever + DPIIT subsidy | Desktop-first, English-heavy, form-UX, low paid churn | Mobile-native + conversational |
| **HajariBook** | Cheapest (₹200/user/yr) + face recognition | Construction-focused, manual settings pain | Hinglish voice self-serve |
| **Keka** | Mid-market ($85.9M ARR, ₹6,999+/mo) | Not designed for 5-person shops | Priced-out segment is our TAM |
| **Khatabook** | 10M+ MAU free ledger + dead Pagarkhata payroll (2020–2025) | Form-based payroll, free+free=no revenue, wrong sequencing (ledger→staff) | Daily attendance drives weekly retention, not monthly ledger |
| **PagarBook** | ₹24.8M raised, $127M valuation (2020), 85% down-round (2025) | Spent $20M buying free downloads; tried lending on chaos | Monetize AI chaos-cleanup, not credit |
| **factoTime** | Bootstrapped attendance | Missing leaves; tax-form gaps | Full compliance |
| **Meta WhatsApp Business AI** (May 2026) | ₹0.78/conversation customer chat | No HR/payroll product; horizontal plumbing | Vertical compliance moat |

## Pattern insight

Khatabook + Pagarbook + OkCredit combined **burned ₹1,000 Cr to earn ₹240 Cr**. Free distribution + bolt-on monetization is dead. Lessons:

1. **Subscription requires UPI AutoPay** — solved Nov 2025 at 1.27B mandates. Didn't exist when Khatabook tried.
2. **Vertical-workflow-lock beats horizontal-ledger** — daily attendance creates muscle memory; monthly ledger doesn't.
3. **Lending fails on chaos.** Pagarbook, Khatabook, OkCredit all tried lending without clean operational data. EWA on Arth Saathi's payroll data is fundamentally different.
