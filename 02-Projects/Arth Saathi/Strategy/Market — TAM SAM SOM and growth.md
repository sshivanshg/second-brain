# Market — TAM, SAM, SOM and growth

## The numbers

| Layer | Size | Source |
|------|------|--------|
| **TAM** | 78.3M Udyam-registered MSMEs (Feb 2026) | MoMSME |
| **TAM growth** | 0.79 Cr → 7.83 Cr in 4 years (10×) | FY22–FY26 |
| **SAM** | 15–20 lakh shops (5–50 emp, T1/T2/T3, smartphone owners) | ~2–3% of Udyam |
| **SOM** | 1M paying shops @ ₹600 Cr ARR | 5-yr target, <1% penetration, ₹500 ARPU |

## Segment breakdown (5–50 employee shops in target)
- Kirana / medical — **13M**
- Restaurants / dhabas — **2M**
- Coaching / clinics — **1.5M**
- Boutiques / tailoring — **1.2M**
- Salons / spas — **0.9M**
- Factories / contractors — **0.6M**

## Tailwinds
- **73%** MSMEs report digital growth (PayNearby 2025)
- HRMS adoption growing at **15–20% CAGR**
- India HR-tech market: **$1.2B (2025) → $2–4B by 2034**
- **<1k-employee segment = 41%** of India HR-tech in 2025
- **88%** of 64M MSMEs still use Excel/notebook for staff
- **90%+** accept digital payments but only **13%** use digital marketing, **18%** credit, **33%** cloud tools

## What this means
The market is huge and digitally-naive in equal measure. The opportunity is to capture the long-tail of shops the enterprise HRMS players (Keka, Darwinbox) ignore by design — at ₹0 entry, with conversational UX, monetizing via fintech rails.
