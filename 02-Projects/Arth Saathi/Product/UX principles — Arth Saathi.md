# UX principles — Arth Saathi

> Design philosophy. Screens follow these; if a screen violates one, the screen is wrong.

## The 6 principles

### 1. Confirm before write
Every AI mutation requires owner approval via a `ConfirmationCard` component. Klarna's 2024–25 rehiring proved efficiency-only AI fails on edge cases. See [[Confirm before write — AI mutations need human approval]].

### 2. Phone-first
Works on a ₹6,000 phone on 3G. No bloated JS bundles. No assumptions about a desktop fallback.

### 3. Hinglish is the UX, not a toggle
*"Ramesh ki haziri laga do"* via voice/chat replaces a 5-field attendance form. **Forms are the failure mode.** See [[Voice and Hinglish are the UX not a translation]].

### 4. Attendance is the anchor
Daily "who's coming today?" is the engagement gravity well. Every other surface (payroll, billing, lending) flows from owning that 9 AM moment. See [[Daily-active surfaces own monthly-active surfaces]].

### 5. Employee app is virality
Every slip is a billboard to a future owner. Watermark visible, share-ready, language matches employee preference. K-factor ≥1.3 by design. See [[Every salary slip is a billboard — K-factor design]].

### 6. Compliance as moat in UX
Once an owner files PF/ESI/TDS through Arth Saathi, switching means re-onboarding UAN/ESIC/TAN with a competitor — months of pain. Make the compliance flow feel inevitable, not optional. See [[Compliance integration is a structural moat]].
